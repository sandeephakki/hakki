About

Systems are only worth building if they solve a real problem — that's the lens I bring to every architecture decision. I started out fresh from an Electronics & Communications degree, took a bet on a .NET diploma, and wrote my first line of C# back in 2011. Over 15 years that bet turned into a career spanning full-stack development, data engineering, and now enterprise-scale cloud architecture.

Today, as a Solutions Architect at Nokia, I design end-to-end Azure cloud solutions, integrate AI capabilities like Azure OpenAI and Semantic Kernel into existing systems, and act as the technical decision point across cross-functional teams — asking "should we build this" before "how do we build this."

Off the clock, I mentor students on careers in tech, and I'm currently digging into agentic AI systems and how architects — not just engineers — can lead AI adoption inside large organisations.
